# MomenRia

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Indonesian-speaking people who want a portrait with family or loved ones when they have separate photos rather than a shared sitting. Celebrations and graduation are supported occasions.

## Product Purpose

Create an AI portrait together from separate photos. Success means choosing a suitable composition, placing one permitted photo per person, checking every face and saving the result.

## Positioning

The template defines the people count and positions. Per-face review and correction retain immutable versions, so the user can compare changes before saving.

## Operating Context

The user confirmed on 9 October 2026 that the interface should prepare for real private-photo use while launch gates remain enforced. The actual configured processing and access modes determine which warnings the app displays.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Ownership, verified email, consent, scoped staff access, immutable result versions, expiry and processing admission remain enforced. Each account receives three lifetime successfully generated new pictures, then pays IDR9,900 per new picture before generation. Corrections to the same picture, greetings and downloads stay free. All free/paid downloads use the full-resolution master without a product watermark; display thumbnails remain lightweight. New paid checkout is paused until Midtrans and operational release gates close. Synthetic results and shared demos must be visibly labeled. Provider privacy, provider deletion evidence, real likeness benchmarks and the operating team's response arrangements remain release questions requiring evidence.

## Brand Commitments

Retain MomenRia's warm Indonesian voice and existing photo scrapbook identity. Describe concrete actions and actual product limits.

## Evidence on Hand

The catalog contains 28 templates across six collections. Template art and tests/e2e/generated face fixtures are synthetic illustrations, not permissioned customer output, testimonials or likeness evidence. The approved audit is docs/design-audit-2026-10-09/README.md.

## Product Principles

- Keep private-photo ownership and consent visible at the point of use.
- Let people inspect every face and compare immutable results.
- Preserve work and offer a real recovery action when something fails.
- Report actual operating mode, expiry and processing status without invented promises.
- Treat interface polish and production readiness as separate evidence requirements.
